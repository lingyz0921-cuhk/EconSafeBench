# Artifact Terms

This artifact is provided for anonymous peer review and research
reproducibility.

## Scope

- Code in `src/` is released under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.
- Benchmark data in `data/` and raw evaluation logs in `results/` are provided
  for non-commercial research, review, and reproducibility.
- The benchmark contains synthetic economic scenarios and simulated account,
  client, worker, and platform states. It is not financial, legal, HR, lending,
  or pricing advice.

## Intended Use

- Reproduce the paper's reported aggregate metrics from the included raw logs.
- Inspect executable economic environments, rule-based judges, and LLM-judge
  rubrics.
- Evaluate future models in a controlled research setting.

## Out-of-Scope Use

- Deploying the scenarios or judgments as real-world compliance tools.
- Using generated model outputs as financial advice or employment decisions.
- Re-identification attempts, even though the dataset is synthetic.
- Commercial redistribution of the benchmark data without explicit permission
  from the authors.

## Required Attribution

For anonymous review, cite the submission as:

> Anonymous Authors. 2026. When Helpful Agents Cause Financial Harm:
> Evaluating LLM Agent Safety in Executable Economic Worlds. Under review.

After de-anonymization, replace this citation with the final publication.
